# AI-assisted MRI Papers

## 1. Lung Imaging with UTE MRI
- **Authors**: Peder E. Z. Larson
- **Summary**: This paper discusses the challenges and advantages of using MRI for lung imaging, specifically with UTE MRI. It covers pulse sequences, motion management methods, and contrast mechanisms used in imaging the lung.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00381v1)
- **Published**: December 1, 2023

## 2. Joint CS-MRI Reconstruction and Segmentation with a Unified Deep Network
- **Authors**: Liyan Sun, Zhiwen Fan, Yue Huang, Xinghao Ding, John Paisley
- **Summary**: This study introduces a unified deep neural network (SegNetMRI) for combined CS-MRI reconstruction and segmentation, improving performance in both areas via compressive measurements.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.02165v1)
- **Published**: May 6, 2018

## 3. Deep Attentive Wasserstein Generative Adversarial Networks for MRI Reconstruction with Recurrent Context-Awareness
- **Authors**: Yifeng Guo, Chengjia Wang, Heye Zhang, Guang Yang
- **Summary**: The paper presents a method coupling WGAN with RNN to improve CS-MRI reconstruction by better utilizing k-space information and reducing residual noise.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.12915v1)
- **Published**: June 23, 2020

## 4. DDM²: Self-Supervised Diffusion MRI Denoising with Generative Diffusion Models
- **Authors**: Tiange Xiang, Mahmut Yurt, Ali B Syed, Kawin Setsompop, Akshay Chaudhari
- **Summary**: Proposes a self-supervised denoising method using diffusion generative models for improving MRI quality without supervision, focusing on noise reduction in diffusion MRI.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.03018v1)
- **Published**: February 6, 2023

## 5. Deep Dive into MRI: Exploring Deep Learning Applications in 0.55T and 7T MRI
- **Authors**: Ana Carolina Alves, André Ferreira, Behrus Puladi, Jan Egger, Victor Alves
- **Summary**: Reviews how DL techniques are integrated into advanced MRI modalities (0.55T and 7T) to enhance detail preservation and tissue characterization.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.01318v1)
- **Published**: July 1, 2024

## 6. Pseudo-MRI-Guided PET Image Reconstruction Method Based on a Diffusion Probabilistic Model
- **Authors**: Weijie Gan, Huidong Xie, Carl von Gall, et al.
- **Summary**: Describes a method using inferred MRI to guide PET reconstruction, improving image quality using a diffusion probabilistic model.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.18139v1)
- **Published**: March 26, 2024

## 7. Fast MRI Reconstruction Using Deep Learning-based Compressed Sensing: A Systematic Review
- **Authors**: Mojtaba Safari, Zach Eidex, Chih-Wei Chang, Richard L. J. Qiu, Xiaofeng Yang
- **Summary**: This review covers DL-based CS-MRI techniques aimed at accelerating MRI without compromising image quality, highlighting various approaches and future directions in medical imaging.
- **PDF**: [Download PDF](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.00241v1)
- **Published**: April 30, 2024
