# AI-Assisted MRI Research Papers

Here are some recent papers related to AI-assisted MRI:

1. **[Lung Imaging with UTE MRI](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00381v1)**
   - **Authors:** Peder E. Z. Larson
   - **Summary:** This paper discusses the intricacies of lung imaging using MRI, specifically focusing on the benefits of UTE MRI for imaging the lung's fine structures without ionizing radiation. It covers pulse sequences, motion management methods, image reconstruction, and contrast mechanisms of UTE MRI.
   - **Published:** 2023-12-01

2. **[Joint CS-MRI Reconstruction and Segmentation with a Unified Deep Network](http://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.02165v1)**
   - **Authors:** Liyan Sun, Zhiwen Fan, Yue Huang, Xinghao Ding, John Paisley
   - **Summary:** The study introduces SegNetMRI, a unified deep neural network architecture designed to enhance both MRI reconstruction and segmentation through shared encoder networks.
   - **Published:** 2018-05-06

3. **[Deep Attentive Wasserstein Generative Adversarial Networks for MRI Reconstruction with Recurrent Context-Awareness](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.12915v1)**
   - **Authors:** Yifeng Guo, Chengjia Wang, Heye Zhang, Guang Yang
   - **Summary:** This paper presents a novel deep learning-based method that employs Wasserstein GAN with RNNs for MRI reconstruction, aiming to improve results and reduce noise artefacts compared to traditional CS-MRI methods.
   - **Published:** 2020-06-23

4. **[DDM$^2$: Self-Supervised Diffusion MRI Denoising with Generative Diffusion Models](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.03018v1)**
   - **Authors:** Tiange Xiang, Mahmut Yurt, Ali B Syed, Kawin Setsompop, Akshay Chaudhari
   - **Summary:** This study proposes a self-supervised method for denoising diffusion MRI using generative diffusion models, significantly improving denoising performance without requiring supervised training datasets.
   - **Published:** 2023-02-06

5. **[Deep Dive into MRI: Exploring Deep Learning Applications in 0.55T and 7T MRI](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.01318v1)**
   - **Authors:** Ana Carolina Alves, André Ferreira, Behrus Puladi, Jan Egger, Victor Alves
   - **Summary:** The paper explores the integration of deep learning in different MRI modalities like 0.55T and 7T, emphasizing enhanced image detail and tissue characterization.
   - **Published:** 2024-07-01

6. **[Pseudo-MRI-Guided PET Image Reconstruction Method Based on a Diffusion Probabilistic Model](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.18139v1)**
   - **Authors:** Weijie Gan, Huidong Xie, Carl von Gall, Günther Platsch, Michael T. Jurkiewicz, Andrea Andrade, Udunna C. Anazodo, Ulugbek S. Kamilov, Hongyu An, Jorge Cabello
   - **Summary:** This work exploits diffusion probabilistic models to generate MRI-like guidance for PET reconstruction, improving image quality even without direct MRI data.
   - **Published:** 2024-03-26

7. **[Fast MRI Reconstruction Using Deep Learning-based Compressed Sensing: A Systematic Review](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.00241v1)**
   - **Authors:** Mojtaba Safari, Zach Eidex, Chih-Wei Chang, Richard L. J. Qiu, Xiaofeng Yang
   - **Summary:** This systematic review analyzes various DL-based compressed sensing techniques for accelerating MRI, summarizing their impact on improving imaging speed while maintaining quality.
   - **Published:** 2024-04-30

These papers reflect various advancements in MRI techniques enhanced by AI, primarily focusing on improving imaging quality, reconstruction speed, and the integration of AI methods like deep learning and GANs into medical imaging.
